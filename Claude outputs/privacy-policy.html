Effective Date: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Rajakiya ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Rajakiya is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect minimal information:

  • No personal information: We do not require account creation, sign-up, login, or any personal details such as name, email, phone number, or address.
  • No authentication data: The app does not collect passwords, tokens, or any form of identity credentials.
  • Language preference: Your selected language is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to any server.
  • Cached content: News articles and educational content are cached locally on your device for offline access. This data stays on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To be explicit, Rajakiya does NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data (GPS, IP-based location)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • Browsing history or search history
  • Financial or payment information
  • Biometric data
  • Health or fitness data

3. How We Use Information

The app fetches publicly available educational content from our content repository hosted on GitHub. This content delivery uses standard HTTPS requests. No user-identifying information is sent with these requests beyond what is standard in any internet connection (such as IP address in server logs, which we do not store or analyze).

4. Third-Party Services

Rajakiya uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub (Content Delivery): Educational articles and news content are fetched from a public GitHub repository. GitHub's privacy policy applies to their infrastructure: GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do NOT use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or social media SDKs.

5. Data Storage

All app data (language preference, cached articles, theme settings) is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. No data is stored on external servers by us. You can clear all app data at any time through your device's Settings → Apps → Rajakiya → Clear Data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to share.

7. Children's Privacy

Rajakiya is an educational app suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, no special provisions for children's data are necessary.

8. Security

All content fetched by the app is transmitted over secure HTTPS connections. Since we do not collect or store personal data on servers, risks associated with data breaches of personal information do not apply.

9. Content Disclaimer

Rajakiya is an independent educational application. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government entity, ministry, or official body of the Government of India. All content is compiled from publicly available official sources (such as india.gov.in, sansad.in, sci.gov.in, eci.gov.in, and legislative.gov.in) for educational purposes only. Users should refer to official government sources for legal or administrative purposes. Some article images are AI-generated for illustrative purposes.

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, traditional data-subject rights (access, correction, deletion) are not applicable. However, you can:

  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored data
  • Clear app cache and data from your device settings
  • Choose your preferred language within the app

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
