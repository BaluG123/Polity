# TargetPolity Play Store Listing

## Short Description

Learn Indian Polity, Constitution, landmark cases, maps and UPSC/KPSC quizzes.

## Long Description

TargetPolity is an Indian Polity learning app built for UPSC, KPSC, state PSC, SSC, banking, law entrance and general studies learners who want to understand the Constitution instead of memorising random notes.

Explore the Indian Constitution, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Parliament, President, government structure, judiciary, federalism, elections, landmark Supreme Court cases and important constitutional amendments in one focused app.

Use the India events map to connect polity topics with real historical context. See key constitutional events, independence milestones, federal developments, rights-based judgments, election reforms and amendment timelines in a visual way that improves recall.

Practice with offline quiz levels, explanations and exam-style questions. Sign in with Google to sync progress, quiz points and leaderboard ranking.

Key features:

- Indian Constitution notes and article explanations
- Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Parliament and President topics
- Union, State and Local Government learning sections
- Judiciary, Supreme Court, High Courts, PIL and judicial review topics
- Landmark cases including Kesavananda Bharati, Maneka Gandhi, Vishaka and privacy judgments
- Indian political history and constitutional events map
- Important amendments, federalism and election milestones
- Offline polity quiz practice with explanations
- Progress tracking, bookmarks and leaderboard
- Dark mode and light mode
- Multi-language app interface with verified lesson translations planned gradually

TargetPolity is useful for:

- UPSC Civil Services preparation
- KPSC and other state PSC exams
- SSC, banking and railway general awareness
- Law entrance and constitutional law basics
- Political science students
- Anyone who wants to learn Indian democracy, Constitution and governance

Search topics covered include Indian Polity, Constitution of India, UPSC Polity, KPSC Polity, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, Parliament of India, President of India, Supreme Court cases, landmark judgments, constitutional amendments, federalism, election commission, Panchayati Raj, local government, judiciary and Indian political history.

TargetPolity is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government entity. It is an educational learning app for exam preparation and civic understanding.
