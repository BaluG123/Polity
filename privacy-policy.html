1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Silicon City Apps (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Rajakiya mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo URL (if your Google account provides one)
  • Google user identifier / Firebase Authentication user ID

You can use much of the App without signing in. Sign-in is optional and is used to save bookmarks, reading preferences, and related account features.

2.2 Information created while you study

  • Bookmarks and similar in-app reading activity
  • Language and theme preferences stored on your device

The App does not include a quiz or leaderboard. We do not collect quiz scores.

2.3 Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Firebase installation / device identifiers used to operate Firebase services
  • Network status (to know whether content can be downloaded)
  • Crash and diagnostic information if a failure occurs

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, payment card details, or health data.

3. How we use information

  • To provide App features: lessons, news summaries, bookmarks, optional account sync
  • To authenticate you via Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication
  • To store and sync bookmarks and preferences in Firebase Firestore when you are signed in
  • To send optional notifications about new educational content (with your permission on Android 13+)
  • To fix bugs, improve stability, and understand which features are used
  • To respond if you contact us for support

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to show third-party advertising in the App.

4. Notifications

The App may request permission to post notifications so we can alert you about new educational articles or study updates. Notifications may be delivered using Firebase Cloud Messaging and/or local notification libraries. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

5. Third-party services

We use the following processors to run the App:

  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging — account, cloud storage of app data, and messages. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Notifee — local notifications on your device.

These providers process data on our behalf to operate the App. They may process data on servers outside India. We do not share your information with other companies for their independent marketing.

6. Local storage

The App stores preferences, cached educational content, and similar data on your device (for example using on-device storage) so features can work offline after content has been downloaded.

7. Data retention

  • Account and synced bookmarks/preferences: kept while your account exists
  • On-device data: remains until you clear app data or uninstall the App
  • Crash/diagnostic logs: kept only as long as needed to investigate issues

8. Your choices and rights

  • You may use the App without Google Sign-In.
  • You may revoke Google access from your Google account permissions.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you by emailing us.
  • You may uninstall the App to remove local data from that device.

Users in the EEA/UK and residents of California have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA (access, deletion, portability, and, where applicable, objection). We do not sell personal information as defined under CCPA.

If you are in India, you may also contact us regarding access and correction requests under applicable Indian data protection law.

9. Children’s privacy

Rajakiya is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS for network traffic to our cloud services and rely on Google Firebase security controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please keep your Google account protected.
