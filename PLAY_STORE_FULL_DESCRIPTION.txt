Rajakiya is a private study companion for UPSC and State PSC aspirants, law students, political science learners, and citizens who want Indian polity explained in simple language.

WHAT YOU CAN LEARN
- Daily polity news cards: unofficial educational summaries of Parliament, courts, elections, policy, and constitutional topics (not official press releases)
- Government structure: Executive, Legislature, and Local Bodies (Panchayati Raj, Municipalities)
- Judiciary: court hierarchy, landmark cases summarised for study, Fundamental Rights, DPSP, writs
- Constitution study notes: Articles by Parts, Schedules in plain language, amendments, Preamble, Fundamental Duties — this is NOT the official Gazette text
